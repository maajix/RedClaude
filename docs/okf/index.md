# redKraken hunting corpus

The shipped Playbook, Skill and maintainer-reference catalogue as a Google Open Knowledge Format v0.2 bundle. Every concept here is derived from the same compiled corpora the runtime reads and is regenerated by `redkraken.okf.build`; nothing in this directory is authored by hand, and nothing in it is loaded by the runtime.

* [playbooks](playbooks/index.md) - 51 Playbooks: what each concludes about, what selects it, and what it owes before a claim moves.
* [skills](skills/index.md) - 6 Skills: the executor half of the graph.
* [references](references/index.md) - 86 maintainer references, one concept each.
